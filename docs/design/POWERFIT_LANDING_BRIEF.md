# PowerFit Landing Brief

This brief defines the content and storytelling structure for the PowerFit public landing page. The page must sell PowerFit as a premium dark fitness SaaS for personal trainers and students while preserving the current PowerFit palette.

## Landing Goal

Convert personal trainers and students into account creation by clearly showing that PowerFit replaces scattered management with one connected system for workouts, students, schedule, progress, body targets, and contextual AI.

The landing should make the product feel real and already usable, not like a generic future SaaS concept.

## Target User

Primary user:

- Personal trainers who manage several students and need less manual follow-up.
- Trainers using WhatsApp, notes, spreadsheets, and memory to coordinate workouts and progress.
- Trainers who want a premium product experience for students without losing control of the coaching relationship.

Secondary user:

- Students who want their workout, progress, body targets, and AI Personal in one simple app.

## Positioning

PowerFit is a premium operating system for personal training.

Core promise:

- For the trainer: organize students, workouts, routine, progress, and next actions.
- For the student: follow the plan, understand progress, and get contextual AI guidance.
- For both: keep the trainer-student relationship central while using AI as support.

Tone:

- Confident, direct, professional, PT-BR.
- Avoid hype and generic startup language.
- Prefer concrete product value over abstract claims.

## Section Structure

The landing must follow this structure:

1. Hero
2. Do caos ao controle
3. Painel do Personal
4. App do Aluno
5. IA Personal contextual
6. Evolucao e Alvos Corporais
7. Free vs Pro/VIP
8. CTA final

## Hero Composition

Hero should establish the whole product quickly.

Content:

- Brand lockup.
- Compact premium badge for personal trainers.
- Strong headline about treinos, alunos, evolucao, agenda, and IA Personal in one app.
- Short paragraph explaining the trainer-student connected flow.
- Two CTAs: `Sou Personal Trainer` and `Sou Aluno`.
- Proof row with three short benefits.

Visual:

- Large dark product frame.
- Split between trainer management and student app cards.
- Floating cards for schedule, AI, or weekly adherence.
- Dark grid and orange/blue ambient glows only.

Copy direction:

- `Treinos, alunos, evolucao e IA Personal em um so app`
- `O PowerFit conecta o painel do personal com a rotina real do aluno: treino, agenda, evolucao, alvos corporais e IA contextual no mesmo fluxo.`

## Product Storytelling

The landing should explain the system as a connected flow:

- Trainer creates and adjusts the plan.
- Student follows workout and records progress.
- Evolution and body targets turn into evidence.
- AI Personal uses real context to help the student stay on track.
- Trainer keeps control and visibility.

Use dashboard snippets, cards, rows, and metrics to show this instead of long text blocks.

## Personal Dashboard Section

Purpose:

- Show PowerFit as an organized management layer for trainers.

Must communicate:

- Student list and status.
- Workout delivery and weekly adherence.
- Next action per student.
- Schedule or session confirmation.

Visual direction:

- Attio-like organized dashboard density, but with PowerFit colors.
- Dark table/list rows with orange active state and blue data markers.
- Small CRM-like tags such as `Hoje`, `Ajustar carga`, `4x semana`, `Meta ativa`.

Copy direction:

- `Painel do Personal`
- `Veja quem treinou, quem precisa de ajuste e qual aluno exige atencao hoje.`

## Student App Section

Purpose:

- Show that the student gets a premium, simple companion app.

Must communicate:

- Today's workout.
- Exercise structure.
- Weekly plan.
- Progress and guidance.

Visual direction:

- Mobile-like dark cards.
- Optional training photo as a dark contextual block, never a white fitness template.
- Workout card should be primary orange-accented; progress card can use blue/cyan.

Copy direction:

- `App do Aluno`
- `O aluno abre o treino, acompanha a semana e entende o progresso sem depender de mensagens soltas.`

## AI Personal Section

Purpose:

- Explain AI as contextual coaching support inside the product.

Must communicate:

- AI reads workout/progress/body target context.
- AI supports consistency and interpretation.
- AI does not replace the trainer.

Visual direction:

- Embedded dark chat panel.
- Context chips above messages: `70kg`, `15% gordura`, `4x semana`, `Foco: peito + core`.
- Blue/cyan can signal intelligence; orange keeps brand ownership.

Copy direction:

- `IA Personal contextual`
- `A IA responde com base no plano, nas medidas e no historico do aluno, mantendo o personal no centro.`

## Evolution And Body Targets Section

Purpose:

- Show progress as measurable and visual.

Must communicate:

- Weight, body fat, weekly training, and target focus.
- Before/after style should be avoided unless implemented tastefully.
- Body targets should feel like goal tracking, not gimmick.

Visual direction:

- Metrics, bars, small chart cards, target body zones, progress states.
- Use orange for current focus and blue/cyan for analytical data.

Copy direction:

- `Evolucao e Alvos Corporais`
- `Medidas, graficos e alvos ajudam personal e aluno a transformar progresso em decisao pratica.`

## Free vs Pro/VIP Section

Purpose:

- Clarify access tiers without making Free feel broken.

Must communicate:

- Free keeps essential dashboard and workouts from trainer.
- Pro/VIP adds AI Personal and autonomy with body targets.
- The trainer-student relationship remains intact.

Visual direction:

- Dark comparison card with clear checks.
- Pro/VIP column gets orange emphasis.
- Avoid pricing complexity unless pricing exists elsewhere.

Copy direction:

- `Comece com o essencial. Evolua para autonomia com IA.`
- `O aluno normal continua recebendo treinos do personal. O Pro/VIP adiciona IA Personal e alvos corporais sem quebrar o vinculo com o treinador.`

## Final CTA

Purpose:

- End with a focused conversion moment.

Visual direction:

- Large dark card with soft orange glow.
- One primary CTA.
- Reinforce professionalization and product readiness.

Copy direction:

- `Pronto para profissionalizar sua gestao?`
- `Teste o PowerFit e veja como fica mais facil acompanhar treinos, evolucao, agenda e IA contextual em uma experiencia unica.`

## PT-BR Copy Rules

- Use direct Brazilian Portuguese.
- Keep labels short inside mockups.
- Prefer `personal`, `aluno`, `treino`, `evolucao`, `alvos`, `agenda`, `IA Personal`.
- Avoid generic claims like `revolucione sua jornada fitness`.
- Avoid overly formal enterprise language.
- Keep product terms consistent: `Personal`, `Aluno`, `IA Personal`, `Pro/VIP`, `Alvos Corporais`.
